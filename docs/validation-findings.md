# Customer Discovery Findings

## Method

Short interviews with 5 waste pickers and 3 households in Accra to test the assumptions behind the original model.

## Finding 1: Households will not pay a pickup fee

Households were open to giving away sorted plastic but resisted paying anything for pickup.

**Change to the model:** pickup is free for households. The cost is covered by fees paid by processors, who benefit from a steadier supply of sorted material.

## Finding 2: Pickers sell through an aggregator

Pickers do not sell independently. They already sell through an informal aggregator (often called the "boss"), who sets the price and handles onward sale.

**Change to the model:** the platform works with aggregators rather than around them. Aggregators become the main account holders on the supply side, and payouts flow through them to pickers.

## Open questions

- Which mobile money networks do pickers and aggregators actually use day to day?
- What volume data do processors need for EPR reporting?
- What is the smallest pilot area where pickup routes are realistic?
